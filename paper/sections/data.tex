\section{Data}
The empirical data are daily S\&P 500 observations from \texttt{yfinance}
\citep{yfinance}, using ticker \texttt{\string^GSPC} from 2010-01-01 onward.
The analysis uses
\[
  y_t = \log(P_t),
\]
where $P_t$ is the adjusted close price when available and the close price
otherwise. The raw and processed data are cached under \texttt{paper/data/} so
that the experiment does not require a download on every run.
